HALLELUJAH MINISTRIES VADDESWARAM — DAILY TELUGU BIBLE POSTER SYSTEM

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- A mobile-friendly daily poster website.
- 365 day-specific Telugu Bible references and individual online Bible links.
- Daily poster shows the reference only (no copied or invented scripture wording).
- Download poster as PNG and share from mobile.
- Date selector for preview/testing.
- QR generator to create 12 monthly QR images after website hosting.

BIBLE SOURCE
The references link to the Telugu O.V. Bible (BSI), TELUBSI, on YouVersion/Bible.com.
Example verified link: https://www.bible.com/bible/1787/JHN.3.16.TELUBSI
Bible Society of India identifies its Telugu Bible and its partnership with YouVersion.
The full scripture text is not reproduced in these poster images; the user taps the link to read it online.

IMPORTANT
Final monthly QR codes require the final public HTTPS hosting URL. They cannot be correctly generated until the site has been published.
The references are an editorially curated daily reading schedule. Check the reference spreadsheet against the edition used by your church before mass printing.

FREE HOSTING (GITHUB PAGES)
1. Create a free GitHub account.
2. Create a PUBLIC repository named telugu-daily-bible-posters.
3. Upload the contents of this folder: index.html, qr-maker.html, and data/daily-references.json.
4. In Settings → Pages, select Deploy from a branch, choose main and /(root), then Save.
5. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish a URL like https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/telugu-daily-bible-posters/
6. Open the published URL on a phone. It should display today's daily poster for the current month.
7. Open qr-maker.html in a browser, paste the published URL, generate and download all 12 QR images.
8. Test each monthly QR before printing.

MONTHLY QR BEHAVIOUR
The monthly QR carries ?month=1 through ?month=12. It displays that month's verse for today's day number (e.g. May QR on May 5 displays May 5). The preview dropdown lets you check other days. Use the QR only during its corresponding month for the expected daily experience.

POSTER DESIGN
The poster includes the ministry name, Telugu heading, date, reference, and a button/link to read the full verse. It does not display verse text, to avoid reproducing copyrighted scripture text without permission.
